# Landing Page Conversion

Help the right visitor understand the offer and take a useful next action.

## Context

Read the existing site, brand, product facts, target audience, main action, and available proof. Ask only for material facts the user has not supplied and the project cannot establish.

## Build or improve

1. Diagnose the top few obstacles before adding sections.
2. Make the offer and intended audience clear in the opening view.
3. Choose one primary action and give secondary actions less visual emphasis.
4. Add explanation, proof, pricing, objections, and a final action only where real material supports them.
5. Use specific language in the site's own voice. Preserve exact prices, capabilities, and commitments.
6. Prefer real product imagery, examples, or a useful demonstration over decorative filler.
7. Keep same-page CTAs when they help visitors reach pricing, a form, or relevant detail. Do not force them onto every page.

## Honesty and usability

Do not invent testimonials, logos, customer counts, awards, urgency, or guarantees. Mark missing source material explicitly.

Use real form labels, understandable errors, accessible focus, and clear submission feedback. Avoid fake scarcity and obstructive consent or cancellation patterns.

## Verify

Check the opening view at representative phone and desktop sizes, all links, form success/failure states, and performance. When measurement is requested, use a consent-aware tracking plan rather than adding trackers by default.
