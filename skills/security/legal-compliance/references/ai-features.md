# AI Features

Read when user data reaches a model, generated content is presented, or AI can act on a user's behalf.

Map provider, inputs, retention/training settings, subprocessors, output use, tool permissions, and the effect of errors. Product labels alone do not establish a provider's actual data handling.

Determine whether disclosure, transparency, impact assessment, high-risk, or sector-specific requirements apply under current rules. Verify coverage and effective dates for the product and jurisdiction before stating a legal obligation.

Keep disclosures understandable and accurate. Do not invent model capabilities, clinical evidence, accuracy figures, or regulatory approval.

Constrain tools using normal authorization independent of the model. Treat user documents, retrieved content, and provider output as untrusted input where they can influence actions or rendering.

Sources for investigation include the [EU AI Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng), [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), and the actual provider's current contractual and technical documentation.
