# Consent and Tracking

Read before enabling analytics, pixels, replay, advertising, or client storage.

Inventory what is stored or accessed on a device, what data leaves it, purposes, providers, retention, and affected jurisdictions. Classify strictly necessary operation separately from optional measurement or advertising based on the actual rules.

Cookieless does not automatically mean exempt. Internal IDs are not automatically anonymous. Server-side forwarding does not remove the need to assess the original collection and processing.

Where consent is required, verify:
- Optional processing is blocked until an appropriate choice.
- Rejecting and withdrawing are usable.
- Choices persist appropriately and apply consistently.
- Withdrawal stops future processing where required.
- Notices accurately describe providers, purposes, and controls.
- Replay and URL parameters do not leak sensitive information.

Test real network requests before acceptance, after rejection, after acceptance, and after withdrawal. Do not judge compliance from the banner's appearance alone.

For EU/Sweden consult current [ePrivacy legislation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2002/58/oj/eng), [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), [PTS](https://pts.se/), and [IMY](https://www.imy.se/). Verify national implementation and relevant exemptions.
