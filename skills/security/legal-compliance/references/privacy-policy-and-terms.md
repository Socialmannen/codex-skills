# Privacy Notices and Terms

Read when drafting notices, terms, rights handling, or deletion flows.

Establish the operator's identity, actual processing purposes, data categories, legal bases where applicable, recipients, transfers, retention, contact routes, and user rights.

Use product facts and verified provider settings. Do not invent a company address, processing basis, retention period, vendor promise, or legal conclusion.

Distinguish account deletion, legal retention, backups, anonymization, and cancellation of a paid plan. Verify which data is actually removed or retained and what the user is told.

Terms need the real service scope, payment and renewal facts, permitted use, support, termination, and applicable mandatory consumer protections. Avoid a universal template applied without context.

Check applicability against current [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), [IMY](https://www.imy.se/), and the relevant consumer authorities, such as [Konsumentverket](https://www.konsumentverket.se/).
