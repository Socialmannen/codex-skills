# Subscriptions and Trials

Read when recurring payments, free trials, renewal, or cancellation are involved.

Establish the actual merchant, provider role, consumer/business audience, jurisdictions, price, currency, tax presentation, trial conversion, renewal, cancellation, withdrawal, and refund rules.

Verify current requirements and exceptions before asserting a deadline or mandatory interaction. The status and scope of particular click-to-cancel rules can change; do not treat a historical rule summary as current law.

Check that the real checkout and account UI explain the commitment and provide a usable cancellation route. Keep optional retention offers easy to bypass.

Test the provider's sandbox lifecycle, webhook handling, repeated events, entitlement changes, cancellation timing, and records shown to the user.

Consult the relevant current consumer authority, including [Konsumentverket](https://www.konsumentverket.se/), [EU consumer rights legislation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/83/oj/eng), or the [FTC legal library](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library) when applicable.
