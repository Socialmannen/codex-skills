# Vendors and Transfers

Read when an external provider receives personal data or can access it.

Map each provider's purpose, data categories, controller/processor role, hosting location, subprocessors, retention, deletion, and contractual terms. Include auth, payments, analytics, email, fonts/CDNs, support widgets, and AI providers when relevant.

Verify the actual configuration rather than relying on a provider's marketing label or region selector. Identify applicable processing agreements, transfer mechanisms, and transparency requirements from current law.

Check that application logs, support tools, and backups follow the stated policy. Record unavailable contracts or settings as unknown.

For EU/Swedish processing, start from [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) and current [IMY guidance](https://www.imy.se/). Scope other jurisdictions separately.
