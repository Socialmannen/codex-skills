# User Content

Read when users can upload, publish, share, or comment.

Map audience, sharing defaults, access control, content types, moderation tools, abuse reports, removal requests, retention, and appeals. Private collaboration and public hosting can have different obligations.

Verify relevant hosting/intermediary, copyright, illegal-content, privacy, and safety requirements for the actual jurisdiction and service type. Do not reuse a universal removal deadline without checking its legal scope.

Implement the requested reporting and removal flow so it works for the people who need it. Verify permissions, notifications, audit records, and handling of shared or cached copies.

Distinguish required legal processes from recommended moderation policies. Record who handles escalations and what the system actually supports.

Primary starting points include the [EU Digital Services Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj/eng), [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), and the relevant national authority.
